FAQ's

What is zerOvt, exactly?

zerOvt (short for Zero Overthinking) is your pocket-sized reset button for whenever life gets a little too loud. Think of it as a cozy digital space to check your vibe, dump your messy thoughts, catch your breath, and tune out to good sounds.

Do I need to create an account or pay to use it?

Nope! No sign-ups, no passwords to remember, and no boring rules. Just jump straight in whenever your brain needs a quick break—completely free.

How do the 4 features actually work?

Mood Checker: A quick, judgment-free check-in to see how you're really feeling today. Zero Thoughts: A safe space to brain-dump all your overthinking so it stops living rent-free in your head. Zero Pace: Simple breathing exercises to help you slow down and reset your rhythm. Zero Noise: Calming ambient sounds (like rain, ocean waves, and forest vibes) to drown out the daily chaos.

Is my brain-dump / data saved anywhere?

Not at all. Since there are no accounts or logins, whatever you type in Zero Thoughts stays completely private in your session. Once you close or clear it, it’s gone—just like letting go of the stress.
